## Problem

Linear shows how far the work has gone: issues closed, project progress, initiative
health. A goal is usually stated as a number to move, such as an activation rate or a
response time, and that number lives in another tool. Whether the work moved it is
answered outside Linear and joined to the plan by hand.

## Proposal

Tie goals to metrics inside Linear, and roll them up the way work is already organised:
from issues to projects to initiatives. An initiative would then show both the progress
of its work and the movement of the metric it was meant to change.

This proposal has no spec yet. Which metrics, where their values come from and how they
combine on the way up are questions for its first spec.
